Privacy Policy - FactZap

Last updated: May 17, 2026

1. Information Collection

FactZap is a personal automation tool that processes content the user provides. We do not collect or store personal data on third-party servers.

2. Authentication Data

OAuth tokens for YouTube and TikTok are stored locally on the user's machine and never shared.

3. Third-Party Services

The application uses Google Gemini, YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API, Pexels, and Pixabay. Use is subject to their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Storage

All generated content, scripts, and metadata are stored locally on the user's machine. No data is transmitted to FactZap servers (none exist).

5. Cookies

The dashboard runs locally on http://localhost:5000 and does not use third-party cookies.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy at any time. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance.
